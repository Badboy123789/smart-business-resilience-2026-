WHO WE ARE

Engineering With Purpose.

Smart Business Resilience (SBR) focuses on engineering disciplines that contribute to the effective operation and performance of buildings and their systems.

Our areas of focus include electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, public health engineering, lighting and sustainability.

We recognise that successful engineering requires thoughtful consideration of technical requirements, coordination between systems and the needs of the people who use the built environment.
