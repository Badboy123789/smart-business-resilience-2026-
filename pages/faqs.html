What does Smart Business Resilience do?

Smart Business Resilience focuses on engineering-related building services and the considerations involved in coordinated, functional and sustainable building design. Please contact the team to confirm the services relevant to your project.

Which engineering disciplines are covered?

The areas presented on this website include electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, public health engineering, lighting and sustainability. Contact the company to discuss your particular requirements.

Can I enquire about a new project?

Yes. Use the contact page to describe your project, its location, the type of building and the engineering support you are looking for.

What information should I provide in my enquiry?

It is helpful to include the project type, location, current stage, required services, key deadlines and any available drawings or project information.

Does the company consider sustainability?

Sustainability considerations featured on this website include energy efficiency, lighting performance, resource use, maintainability and long-term building performance. The appropriate considerations depend on each project.

Can engineering disciplines be considered together?

Electrical, mechanical, public health, lighting and sustainability considerations can interact. Coordination between relevant disciplines is an important consideration in building services design.

How do I contact Smart Business Resilience?

You can use the contact page or email info@smartbusinessresilience.co.uk to make an enquiry.

Where can I see the company's projects?

Visit the Projects page for the project information available on this website. Please contact the company if you need further details about its experience or previous work.

How can I discuss a specific requirement?

Use the contact page to provide a brief description of your requirements. The company can then advise on the appropriate next steps.
